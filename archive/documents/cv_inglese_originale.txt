SUMMARY
I am a geologist and computer scientist with over 18 years of experience in the utility and IT industry, with a strong specialization in Geographic Information Systems and as a data analyst.  I have experience in both individual development and as part of larger development teams, managing multiple projects and leading small teams in determining critical points and development progress stages. I am proficient in the English language. I have also attended several graduate courses on geological and environmental risk, mining decommissioning, GIS, and remote sensing data, 
I started working in Rome in 2004 for Environ, dealing with the management of site and laboratory maps and data through GIS systems (ArcGIS and ArcIMS), and the writing of environmental characterizations. In 2006, I specialized in conducting health risk analysis. In 2008 I joined DBCAD Perugia as a functional analyst of their GIS systems. In my experience there are also site activities with instrumental data acquisition systems and topographic surveys with differential GPS instrumentation, up to the use of artificial intelligence systems for cultural heritage.
